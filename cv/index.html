Hello! My name is Marina Kreibring.

I'm a linguist, translator, and project coordinator with a passion for technology and lifelong learning. I moved from Belarus to Germany in 2024. I'm currently studying Applied Technologyremotely at Brigham Young University–Idaho. Previously, I earned a Bachelor's degree in Teaching English as a Foreign Language from Minsk State Linguistic University after graduating with honors from Borisov Pedagogical College. I speak Russian, Belarusian, English, and German.

I worked as an English teacher for 10 years, teaching students in grades 1 through 8. By adapting teaching methods to different learning styles and incorporating interactive classroom activities and extracurricular English-language events, I created a dynamic, student-centered learning environment that encouraged student engagement and academic progress. This experience strengthened my communication, organization, and classroom management skills while fostering patience and adaptability.

After that, I worked for 10 years as a Project Coordinator for humanitarian initiatives implemented through the NGO ICPA "Sofia" in cooperation with the Welfare Department of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Belarus. I organized and supervised volunteer services and humanitarian projects, coordinated cross-functional teams, managed logistics, and ensured the successful implementation of programs aimed at improving community health and education. In addition to project management, I provided administrative support for international missionaries. This role required close collaboration with local authorities, international partners, and Church leadership while ensuring compliance with legal and administrative requirements.

I hold multiple certifications from BYU-Idaho, including Computer Support (2022), Web and Computer Programming (2023), English as an International Language (2023), System Administration (2025), and Web Development (2026), along with Associate Degrees in Professional Studies and Applied Technology. My experience spans technical support, system administration, and full-stack web development. I build responsive web applications using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Next.js, Node.js, SQL, APIs, and JSON, and have programming experience with Java and Python. I enjoy creating clean, user-friendly interfaces while continuously improving my technical skills. I plan to complete my Bachelor's degree in the near future.

During my years of study and work, I have strengthened my leadership, organizational, communication, and cross-cultural collaboration skills alongside my professional language expertise. I have developed strong skills in troubleshooting, teamwork and collaboration, time management, interpersonal communication, problem-solving, and adaptability. I am an open, reliable, responsible, and team-oriented professional who thrives in dynamic environments.
