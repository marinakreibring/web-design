Overview Audience Design

Target Audience

Our target audience includes a wide range of individuals who appreciate handmade jewelry and accessories. This includes:

  • Local residents looking for unique pieces to add to their collection.
  • Tourists seeking authentic, handcrafted items as souvenirs.
  • Collectors interested in the artistry and craftsmanship of handmade jewelry.
  • Gift shoppers searching for special, one-of-a-kind presents for loved ones.

Personas

Persona 1

John Smith, a 35-year-old professional working in the city. He is married with two children and enjoys collecting unique jewelry pieces as a hobby. He values quality craftsmanship and is willing to invest in handmade items that reflect his personal style.

Persona 2

Sarah Johnson, a 28-year-old entrepreneur who runs her own small business. She is single and enjoys attending local art fairs and markets to discover new designers. She appreciates the story behind each piece and prefers to support independent artisans.

Scenarios

  • A customer is looking for a unique piece of handmade jewelry for a special occasion.
  • A tourist is visiting the area and wants to purchase a locally made accessory as a souvenir.
  • A collector is searching for a specific type of handmade jewelry to add to their collection.
  • A gift shopper is looking for a one-of-a-kind present for a loved one and wants to support local artisans.
